1. 3rd Group of Verbs "RE" — Pure Irregular Verbs

French: Les verbes irréguliers en -RE / Pronunciation: ले वेर्ब इरेगुलिए आँ एर
📌 Complete 3rd Group "RE" Verb Conjugations:
Irregular "-RE" verbs require distinct stem memorization. Each verb table includes all subject pronouns (Je, Tu, Il/Elle/On, Nous, Vous, Ils/Elles) paired with Devanagari phonetics, audio buttons, and bilingual translations.

2. Les Adjectifs Possessifs (Possessive Adjectives) & Selection Logic

French: Les Adjectifs Possessifs / Pronunciation: ले ज़ाद्झ़ेक्तीफ़ पॉस्सेसीफ़

💡 Core Rule & Logic: How to Choose the Correct Possessive Adjective

In French, possessive adjectives do NOT agree with the owner (the subject/possessor). They agree with the gender (masculine/feminine) and number (singular/plural) of the ITEM OWNED (the noun following the adjective).

2-Step Selection Decision Flowchart:

  1. Step 1: Identify Who Owns It (The Possessor):
    • Is it Je (My)? → Use group: mon, ma, mes
    • Is it Tu (Your - informal)? → Use group: ton, ta, tes
    • Is it Il / Elle / Name (His / Her / Its)? → Use group: son, sa, ses
    • Is it Nous (Our)? → Use group: notre, nos
    • Is it Vous (Your - formal/plural)? → Use group: votre, vos
    • Is it Ils / Elles / Names (Their)? → Use group: leur, leurs
  2. Step 2: Look at the Noun Following (The Possessed Object):
    • If the noun is Masculine Singular → Pick the masculine form (mon, ton, son).
    • If the noun is Feminine Singular → Pick the feminine form (ma, ta, sa).
    • If the noun is Plural (Masc or Fem) → Pick the plural form (mes, tes, ses, nos, vos, leurs).

Why do we switch between `mon`, `ma`, and `mes` for "Je"?

Even though the speaker is always Je (I), the word changes based entirely on the item mentioned:

  • Je + père (masculine noun) → mon père (my father / मेरे पिता)
  • Je + mère (feminine noun) → ma mère (my mother / मेरी माँ)
  • Je + parents (plural noun) → mes parents (my parents / मेरे माता-पिता)
