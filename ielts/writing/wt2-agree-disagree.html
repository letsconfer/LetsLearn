1. Agree or Disagree Essay Blueprint & Golden Rules

In an Agree or Disagree essay, the prompt presents a strong statement or opinion and directly asks: "Do you agree or disagree?" You must pick one clear side (either fully Agree or fully Disagree) and defend it across two body paragraphs using logical reasons and concrete examples. इस निबंध में आपको एक स्पष्ट पक्ष (या तो पूरी तरह सहमत या पूरी तरह असहमत) चुनना होता है और दो मजबूत कारणों के साथ उसे साबित करना होता है।

  • Minimum Word Count: Write at least 250 words (Aim for 275–310 words for full development without rushing).
  • Time Allocation: Spend strictly 40 minutes (5 mins planning + 30 mins writing + 5 mins proofreading).
  • 4-Paragraph Discipline:
    • Paragraph 1 (Introduction): > 2 sentences — Rephrase the Topic + Thesis Statement (Clue).
    • Paragraph 2 (Body Paragraph 1 - BP1): Focus exclusively on your 1st Reason (TS 1).
    • Paragraph 3 (Body Paragraph 2 - BP2): Focus exclusively on your 2nd Reason (TS 2).
    • Paragraph 4 (Conclusion): Restate your position and summarize TS 1 & TS 2 without introducing new ideas.
  • Golden Rule — Pick One Clear Side: Never sit on the fence in a direct "Do you agree or disagree?" question. Choose whichever side gives you two stronger, easier-to-explain reasons.

2. Introduction (>2 Sentences) & Thesis Statement Formula

Your introduction must immediately signal your vocabulary range and your exact stance to the examiner: भूमिका (Introduction) कम से कम 2 वाक्यों की होनी चाहिए: पहला वाक्य विषय को अपने शब्दों में लिखें (Rephrase) और दूसरे वाक्य में अपना स्पष्ट मत (Thesis) दें।

Sentence 1: Rephrase Topic

6 Examiner-Approved Opening Hooks

Start your first sentence with one of these structures before paraphrasing the question:

  1. It is true that...
  2. Few of the people think that...
  3. Most of the people think that...
  4. It is often argued that...
  5. It is generally considered that...
  6. No one can deny that...
Sentence 2: Thesis (Clue)

2 Proven Thesis Statement Templates

Option A (With Specific Clues — Recommended):
I agree/disagree with this notion due to / because of [TS 1] and [TS 2].

Option B (Direct Outline):
I agree/disagree with this notion and will explain the reasons in the upcoming paragraphs.

Pro Tip: Naming your two core reasons (TS 1 & TS 2) right in the thesis gives the examiner a clear roadmap and boosts your Task Response score.

3. The 4-Step Body Paragraph Formula (DEF → EXPLAIN → EXAMPLE → RESULT)

Every body paragraph (both BP1 and BP2) must follow a strict 4-step sequence so your ideas never feel underdeveloped or repetitive: प्रत्येक बॉडी पैराग्राफ को इन 4 चरणों में लिखें: 1. मुख्य कारण बताएं (Define), 2. विस्तार से समझाएं (Explain), 3. उदाहरण दें (Example), 4. परिणाम लिखें (Result)।

Step & Role Transition Starters What to Write in This Sentence
Step 1: DEF (Task Statement) BP1: To commence with, / To begin with, / First of all, / To initial,
BP2: Furthermore, / Moreover, / Secondly, / In addition to this, 		State your single core reason (TS 1 in BP1; TS 2 in BP2) clearly in one direct topic sentence.
Step 2: EXPLAIN In other words,
To elaborate it,
To explain it,
To explicate it, 		Unpack why or how your reason works in real life. Contrast what happens with and without it.
Step 3: EXAMPLE For example,
For instance,
To epitome,
To exemplify,
To cite an example, 		Give a specific, realistic example (researchers, professionals, surveys, or everyday workplace scenarios).
Step 4: RESULT / EFFECT Hence,
Therefore,
Thus,
As a result, 		Tie the example back to your main argument and close the paragraph with a logical takeaway.

4. Live Classroom Construction Walkthrough

Classroom Prompt: "Big salary is much more important than job satisfaction. Do you agree or disagree?"

5-Minute Brainstorming Plan Before Writing

  • Side A (If you AGREE — Salary is more important): TS 1: Covers essential living costs & financial security | TS 2: Enables better healthcare, education & lifestyle upgrades.
  • Side B (If you DISAGREE — Job satisfaction is more important): TS 1: Gives inner fulfillment & mental peace | TS 2: Inspires dedication, creativity, and long-term career growth.

Step-by-Step Drafting Breakdown (Choosing "Disagree")

1. Introduction (Rephrase + Thesis Clue):
"It is often argued that it is more advantageous to choose a job with a high wage even if it does not satisfy you at all. I disagree with this notion because job satisfaction gives us inner fulfillment and inspires individuals to work harder."

2. Body Paragraph 1 Built Using (DEF → EXPLAIN → EXAMPLE → RESULT):

  • [DEF]: First of all, job satisfaction gives people a sense of fulfillment that no amount of money can guarantee.
  • [EXPLAIN]: To explain it, if someone is earning a high salary, but feels tensed and compromises with their conscience every day, that person will not enjoy their life, whereas pursuing one's genuine interests brings lasting pleasure and peace of mind.
  • [EXAMPLE]: For example, numerous famous researchers and scientists made their career choices not because of appealing wages, but because they were deeply passionate about discovery and innovation.
  • [RESULT]: Therefore, it is far more important to choose the kind of work that makes you genuinely happy than to merely chase a high paycheck.

5. Complete Solved Model Essay (Full 4-Paragraph Version)

Topic: Big salary is much more important than job satisfaction. Do you agree or disagree?

It is often argued that it is more advantageous to choose a job with a lucrative wage even if it fails to provide personal fulfillment. However, I strongly disagree with this notion because genuine job satisfaction fosters mental well-being and inspires individuals to achieve long-term professional excellence.

First of all, job satisfaction gives people a sense of fulfillment and emotional stability that money alone can never guarantee. To explain it, when an employee earns a hefty paycheck in a role that causes chronic stress or forces them to compromise with their values, their overall quality of life deteriorates rapidly. Conversely, engaging in meaningful work aligned with one's passion generates daily enthusiasm and prevents burnout. For example, numerous renowned researchers and educators intentionally turn down lucrative corporate offers in favor of academic roles simply because they derive deep satisfaction from teaching and discovery. Therefore, prioritizing happiness at work is essential for a balanced and contented life.

Furthermore, enjoying one's profession naturally motivates individuals to work harder and unlock their full creative potential. In other words, employees who are passionate about their responsibilities willingly go the extra mile, acquire new skills, and consistently deliver superior results without needing constant supervision. To cite an example, many successful entrepreneurs and software innovators started their ventures with modest initial incomes, yet their passion drove them to build thriving enterprises that eventually brought financial rewards as well. As a result, intrinsic motivation proves to be a far more reliable engine for sustainable career growth than salary alone.

In conclusion, while a decent income is necessary to meet basic living expenses, I firmly believe that job satisfaction outweighs a high salary because it safeguards mental health and fuels genuine productivity.

Practice Prompts for Self-Attempt

  1. Education & Technology: Online learning is rapidly replacing traditional classroom education, and some believe physical schools will soon become unnecessary. Do you agree or disagree?
  2. Environment & Government: The best way to solve the world's environmental problems is to increase the cost of fuel for cars and other vehicles. Do you agree or disagree?
  3. Society & Health: Fast food advertisements encourage unhealthy eating habits and should be completely banned by the government. Do you agree or disagree?
