1. Exam Overview & Assessment Breakdown

In IELTS General Training Writing Task 1, you are presented with a real-life situation and asked to write a letter requesting information, complaining, apologizing, inviting, or explaining a scenario. जनरल ट्रेनिंग टास्क 1 में आपको किसी वास्तविक स्थिति पर एक पत्र (Letter) लिखना होता है।

  • Time Limit: Spend no more than 20 minutes (Task 1 accounts for 33% of your total Writing score).
  • Word Count: Write at least 150 words (Aim for 165–190 words for optimal development).
  • 3 Bullet Points Rule: Every prompt contains 3 mandatory bullet points. Dedicate one clear Body Paragraph (BP1, BP2, BP3) to each bullet point.
  • 4 Marking Criteria (25% each): Task Achievement, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource (Vocabulary), and Grammatical Range & Accuracy.
  • Strict No-Contraction Rule in Formal Letters: Never use shortened forms (like don't, I'm, can't) in Formal or Semi-Formal letters.

2. The 3 Letter Styles: Salutations, Skeleton & Sign-offs

Identifying the recipient immediately determines your tone, greeting, and closing sign-off: पत्र किसे लिखा जा रहा है (Recipient), उसी से पत्र की शैली (Formal, Semi-Formal, Informal) तय होती है।

Style 1: Formal Letter

High Authority / Stranger

Recipient: Manager, Local Newspaper Editor, Insurance Company, Municipal Council, Unknown Official.

Salutation: Dear Sir or Madam,

Paragraph Skeleton:

  • Opening: Introduce yourself & state purpose clearly.
  • BP1: Address Bullet Point 1
  • BP2: Address Bullet Point 2
  • BP3: Address Bullet Point 3
  • Closing Line: I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.

Sign-Off: Yours faithfully, + Full Name

Style 2: Semi-Formal Letter

Known Acquaintance / Professional

Recipient: Local Artist / Playback Singer, Landlord, Colleague, Neighbor, Teacher, RJ.

Salutation: Dear Mr. Sharma, / Dear Ms. Verma, / Dear RJ,

Paragraph Skeleton:

  • Opening: State the purpose politely and warmly.
  • BP1: Explain the occasion / context
  • BP2: Give details / reason for writing
  • BP3: Specify expectations / arrangements
  • Closing Line: I hope to receive a positive response from you soon.

Sign-Off: Yours sincerely, + Full Name

Style 3: Informal Letter

Close Friend / Family Member

Recipient: Best Friend, Cousin, Sibling, Close Relative.

Salutation: Dear Ram, (First Name Only)

Paragraph Skeleton:

  • Opening: Warm personal greeting + purpose of writing.
  • BP1: Address Bullet Point 1 (friendly tone)
  • BP2: Address Bullet Point 2
  • BP3: Address Bullet Point 3
  • Closing Line: Looking forward to catching up with you soon!

Sign-Off: Yours lovingly, / Warm regards, + First Name

3. Class Notes: Golden Rules & High-Scoring Connectors

Crucial Classroom Rules to Remember

  • No Conclusion Paragraph in Task 1: Unlike Task 2 essays, Task 1 does not have a summary/conclusion paragraph—end with a polite closing line and sign-off.
  • Avoid the word "BELOW": Never write "in the chart below" or "listed below"; use formal phrasing instead.
  • Exact Precision vs. Approximation: When exact figures are not given, use qualifiers like Approximately, About, Around, Nearby.
  • Time Ranges: Use From [X] to [Y] or Between [X] and [Y] accurately without mixing prepositions.
  • Reporting Verbs (Academic/Data context): Use Illustrates, Represents, Depicts, Demonstrates, Provides information about (never invent fake synonyms for the word "graph").

Essential Linking Words & Connectors (Coherence & Cohesion)

Sequencing / Starting:
First of all, Firstly, Initially सबसे पहले / शुरुआत में
Adding Information:
Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, In addition to this इसके अलावा / साथ ही
Showing Result / Consequence:
Hence, Therefore, Consequently, As a result इसलिए / परिणामस्वरूप
Contrasting Ideas:
However, On the other hand, Nevertheless हालाँकि / इसके विपरीत

4. Solved Classroom Model Letters & Prompts

Model 1: Formal Letter to Local Newspaper (Saving Public Park)

Prompt: There is a public park near where you live. You have heard that the local council wants to sell this park. Write a letter to a local newspaper. In your letter: (1) introduce yourself, (2) describe the importance of the park, (3) say what action you will take if the council continues with its plan.

Dear Sir or Madam,

My name is Vikramjit Singh, and I am a longtime resident of 341 Ferozepur Road. I am writing to bring to your attention a deeply concerning proposal by the local municipal council to sell the public park situated in our neighborhood. This decision has caused widespread distress among local citizens, as it will lead to severe negative consequences for our community.

Let me explain the situation in greater detail. There are approximately 3,000 residents in my locality who rely heavily on this green space. Around 400 to 500 individuals visit the park daily for jogging, morning walks, and outdoor exercise. Furthermore, this is the sole recreational area where senior citizens gather to socialize and children play safely. If the park is sold, residents will be forced to commute to the nearest alternative park located three miles away, which is impractical for elderly individuals on a daily basis.

If the council proceeds with this plan, I will have no option but to organize a peaceful neighborhood petition and escalate the matter to higher authorities. I would be truly grateful if you could publish this issue in your esteemed newspaper to raise public awareness.

Yours faithfully,
Vikramjit Singh

Model 2: Informal Letter to a Friend (Landmark Birthday Celebration)

Prompt: Your friend is about to celebrate a landmark birthday and would welcome suggestions as to how it should be celebrated. Write a letter to your friend. In your letter: (1) say what type of party should be held, (2) suggest a suitable venue, (3) make a recommendation as to entertainment.

Dear Ram,

I hope this letter finds you in high spirits! Since your milestone 30th birthday is right around the corner, I wanted to share a few exciting ideas to make sure we celebrate this unforgettable red-letter day in grand style.

To make this celebration truly unique, I think you should host a vibrant theme-based costume party where all guests dress according to a retro Bollywood or Hollywood theme. A themed gathering instantly breaks the ice and creates a lively atmosphere for everyone attending.

As for the venue, I strongly recommend booking the Orchid View Resort near your residence. It features spacious banquet lawns, exceptional cuisine, and a picturesque ambiance ideal for hosting a large group of friends and family. You should check their availability for next weekend at the earliest.

Moreover, regarding entertainment, we can invite a talented local playback singer to perform live acoustic hits, followed by a DJ session and interactive party games for both youngsters and elders. I am eagerly looking forward to celebrating with you next week!

Yours lovingly,
Vikramjit

Additional Practice Prompts from Class

  • Formal Practice (Travel Insurance Claim): On a recent holiday you lost a valuable item. Fortunately, you have travel insurance to cover the cost of anything lost. Write a letter to the manager of your insurance company. In your letter: (A) describe the item you lost, (B) explain how you lost it, (C) tell the insurance company what you would like them to do.
  • Semi-Formal Practice (Inviting a Local Artist): You are going to arrange a special party at your home next week. You want to invite a local artist who is a playback singer. Write a letter to him. In your letter: (1) explain the occasion, (2) invite him to the party, (3) tell him why you want him to perform.
