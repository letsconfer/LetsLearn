Guide to Vowel Sounds (A, E, I, O, U, Y) & Essential Conversational Expressions

1. French Vowels: A, AI, AU

French: Les Voyelles A, AI, AU / Pronunciation: ले वोयेल्ल आ, ए, ओ
Topic Details / Definition Rules, Logics & Pronunciation Correct Examples Incorrect Examples
Vowel A & Blends (AI, AU) • A: Pronounced like 'a' in ami.
• AI: Pronounced like 'e' in lait (milk).
• AU: Pronounced like eau (ओ).		 • ami (friend) — ah-mee / आमी

• attaque (attack) — ah-tak / आत्ताक

• lait (milk) — lay / ले 		• ami as 'ay-mi'

• lait as 'lat'

2. French Vowels: E & EU Blend

French: La Voyelle E et EU / Pronunciation: ला वोयेल्ल ए ए ओू
Topic Details / Definition Rules, Logics & Pronunciation Correct Examples Incorrect Examples
Vowel E & EU Sound • E: Pronounced like 'uh' in English (unless accented) (e.g., fenêtre, te, me, tasse, jupe).
• EU: Pronounced like 'oo' in bleu.		 • me (me/myself) — muh / म

• tasse (cup) — tas / तास

• bleu (blue) — bluh / ब्ल्यू 		• me as 'mee'

• bleu as 'ble'

3. French Vowel: I

French: La Voyelle I / Pronunciation: ला वोयेल्ल ई
Topic Details / Definition Rules, Logics & Pronunciation Correct Examples Incorrect Examples
Pronounced as "ee" The vowel I in French is consistently pronounced as a clear "ee" (ई) sound. • midi (noon) — mee-dee / मीदी

• lit (bed) — lee / ली

• livre (book) — leevr / लीवर 		• lit as 'liht'

• livre as 'lyv-re'

4. French Vowels: O & OU Blend

French: La Voyelle O et OU / Pronunciation: ला वोयेल्ल ओ ए ओू
Topic Details / Definition Rules, Logics & Pronunciation Correct Examples Incorrect Examples
Vowel O & OU Sound • O: Pronounced like "oh" or 'o' in escargot, rose, loto, mot, trop.
• OU: Pronounced like "oo" in cou (neck).		 • rose (pink/rose) — rohz / रोज़

• trop (too much) — troh / त्रो

• cou (neck) — koo / कू 		• trop as 'trop-pe'

• rose as 'ros-e'

5. French Vowels: U & Y

French: La Voyelle U et Y / Pronunciation: ला वोयेल्ल उ ए ई
Topic Details / Definition Rules, Logics & Pronunciation Correct Examples Incorrect Examples
Vowel U & Y Sound • U: Pronounced like 'eu' in words like rue, jupe, bus, tu, amuse.
• Y: Pronounced like "ee".		 • jupe (skirt) — zhoop / जूप

• tu (you) — too / तू

• bus (bus) — boos / बूस 		• tu as 'taw'

• jupe as 'jup'

6. Essential Expressions: Greetings & Farewells

(Pronunciation: bon-zhoor / बोनजूर) 		Bonjour, comment ça va ? (Hello, how is it going?) Bon jour (two words)

(Pronunciation: bon ah-preh-mee-dee / बोन आप्रे-मीदी) 		Bon après-midi à tous ! (Good afternoon to all!) Good afternoon (English)

English Expression French Translation & Pronunciation Correct Usage Example Incorrect Usage

(Pronunciation: koh-mãh sah vah / कोमाँ सा वा) 		Ça va bien, merci. (It's going well, thank you.) Ca va mal (when happy)

(Pronunciation: koh-mãh vah too / कोमाँ वा तू) 		Salut Paul, comment vas-tu ? (Hi Paul, how are you?) Comment allez-vous to a child
How are you? (Polite) Comment allez-vous ?

(Pronunciation: tray byãh, mair-see / त्रे बियां, मेर्सी) 		Je vais très bien, merci. Tres mal merci
Not bad / So-so Pas mal / Comme ci, comme ça
(Pronunciation: pah mahl / kom see, kom sah / पा माल / कोम सी, कोम सा) 		Ça va, comme ci, comme ça. Comme ça comme ci
Super / Fantastic Super ou formidable
(Pronunciation: soo-pair / for-mee-dahbl / सुपेर / फोर्मीदाब्ल) 		C'est formidable ! (That's fantastic!) Formidable mal
