French Beginner Conversational Guide

Complete Formal vs. Informal Conversations with Questions, Answers, and Pronunciation Guides

How to use this guide: Use Formal (vous) with strangers, elders, or professionals. Use Informal (tu) with friends, family, and peers. Both questions and conversational answers are provided for every topic.
Category / Topic Formal (Polite / Professional) Informal (Casual / Friends)
1. Greeting & How are you
Q: Bonjour, comment allez-vous ? English: Hello, how are you?
Pronunciation:
Hindi: बों-झूर, को-मां ता-ले वू?
English: Bon-zhoor, ko-mah tah-lay voo?
A: Je vais bien, merci. Et vous ? English: I am doing well, thank you. And you?
Pronunciation:
Hindi: जावे व्यान, मेर्सी। ए वू?
English: Zha vay byan, mehr-see. Ey voo?
Q: Salut, ça va ? English: Hi, how is it going?
Pronunciation:
Hindi: सलू, सा वा?
English: Sahlü, sah vah?
A: Ça va bien, et toi ? English: It's going well, and you?
Pronunciation:
Hindi: सा वा ब्यान, ए त्वा?
English: Sah vah byan, ey twa?
2. Asking Name
Q: Comment vous appelez-vous ? English: What is your name?
Pronunciation:
Hindi: को-मां वू-ज़ा-प्ले वू?
English: Ko-mah voo-za-play voo?
A: Je m'appelle [Votre Nom]. Et vous ? English: My name is [Your Name]. And you?
Pronunciation:
Hindi: ज मा-पेल [वो-त्र नोम]। ए वू?
English: Zhe ma-pel [vo-tre nom]. Ey voo?
Q: Tu t'appelles comment ? English: What's your name?
Pronunciation:
Hindi: तू ता-पेल को-मा?
English: Tü ta-pel ko-mah?
A: Moi, c'est [Prénom]. Et toi ? English: Me, it's [First Name]. And you?
Pronunciation:
Hindi: म्वा, से [प्रे-नोम]। ए त्वा?
English: Mwa, sey [pray-nom]. Ey twa?
