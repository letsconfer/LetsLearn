L' (Vowels & Silent H) Used before any singular noun (masculine or feminine) starting with a vowel (a, e, i, o, u) or a silent 'h'. The vowel of Le or La is dropped and replaced with an apostrophe to avoid vowel clash (Elision).

English Comparison: Like using 'the' before words starting with a vowel (though English doesn't drop spelling, like *the apple*).
Hindi Comparison: स्वर से शुरू होने वाले शब्दों के पहले आर्टिकल का संक्षिप्त रूप।		 • l'école (school)
