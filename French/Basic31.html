⚡ Master Cheat Code & Rules

  • 1st Group (-ER): Drop -er and add -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent. Note: -es and -ent are silent. One French form expresses simple, continuous, and emphatic (e.g., Je parle = I speak / I am speaking / I do speak).
  • 2nd Group (-IR): Drop -ir and add -is, -is, -it, -issons, -issez, -issent (featuring the -iss- plural infix).
  • 3rd Group (-RE / Irregular): Includes regular -re verbs and irregular verbs like Prendre, Apprendre, Comprendre, and Aller which require separate memorization.

The 3 Regular Verb Groups in French (Les 3 Groupes de Verbes)

In French, regular verbs are categorized into three main groups based on their infinitive endings: -ER, -IR, and -RE. Each group follows its own set of rules and present-tense conjugation endings.

1. First Group (-ER Verbs) — Parler (To Speak)

Overview: This is the largest and most regular group of verbs in French (about 90% of all French verbs). To conjugate, remove -er to find the stem, then add the endings: -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent.

Common Examples: parler (to speak), aimer (to like/love), regarder (to watch), travailler (to work).

📌 Pronoun & Ending Rule for 1st Group (-ER): Click for 80+ '-ER' Verbs examples
To conjugate any regular -er verb (e.g., parler), drop the -er stem and
apply these pronoun endings:
  • Je (I): Add -e → Je parle (झ़् पर्ल)
  • Tu (You - informal): Add -es (silent) → Tu parles (त्यू पर्ल)
  • Il / Elle / On (He / She / One): Add -e → Il parle (इल पर्ल)
  • Nous (We): Add -ons → Nous parlons (नू पर्लों)
  • Vous (You - formal/plural): Add -ez → Vous parlez (वू पर्ले)
  • Ils / Elles (They): Add -ent (silent) → Ils parlent (इल पर्ल)

2. Second Group (-IR Verbs) — Finir (To Finish)

Overview: Regular -ir verbs (often called finir - type verbs) form the 2nd group. They are characterized by adding an -iss- sound in plural conjugations. Remove -ir and add the endings: -is, -is, -it, -issons, -issez, -issent.

Common Examples: finir (to finish), choisir (to choose), grandir (to grow), réussir (to succeed).

📌 Pronoun & Ending Rule for 2nd Group (-IR):
To conjugate any regular -ir verb (e.g., finir), drop the -ir stem and apply these pronoun endings (incorporating the -iss- infix for plural forms):
  • Je (I): Add -is → Je finis (झ़् फ़िनी)
  • Tu (You - informal): Add -is → Tu finis (त्यू फ़िनी)
  • Il / Elle / On (He / She / One): Add -it → Il finit (इल फ़िनी)
  • Nous (We): Add -issons → Nous finissons (नू फ़िनिसों)
  • Vous (You - formal/plural): Add -issez → Vous finissez (वू फ़िनिसे)
  • Ils / Elles (They): Add -issent → Ils finissent (इल फ़िनीस)

3. Third Group (-RE & Irregular) — Prendre, Apprendre, Comprendre

Overview: Regular -re verbs form a smaller regular group. To conjugate them, remove -re to get the stem, then add the endings: -s, -s, (nothing), -ons, -ez, -ent. Note that the il/elle form takes no additional ending.

Common Examples: vendre (to sell), attendre (to wait for), répondre (to answer), perdre (to lose).

📌 Logic & Conjugation Rule for Prendre & Derivatives (-dre Verbs):
Verbs like prendre (to take) and its family (apprendre = to learn, comprendre = to understand) follow a specific pattern:
  • Singular (Je, Tu, Il/Elle): Keep the 'd' in the stem.
    • Je: Add -s → Je prends (झ़् प्रों) (ends in silent 's').
    • Tu: Add -s → Tu prends (त्यू प्रों) (ends in silent 's').
    • Il / Elle: Drop the 's' (no ending) → Il prend (इल प्रों) (same sound).
  • Plural (Nous, Vous, Ils/Elles): Drop the 'd' entirely and apply standard plural endings:
    • Nous (We): Drop 'd' + -ons → Nous prenons (नू प्रनों)
    • Vous (You): Drop 'd' + -ez → Vous prenez (वू प्रने)
    • Ils / Elles (They): Drop 'd' + double 'nn' + -ent → Ils prennent (इल प्रैन)

    • 💡 The Family Cheat Code:
    Because apprendre and comprendre contain prendre inside them, they conjugate using the exact same rule! Simply attach the prefix (ap- or com-) to the front of each form.

Pronoun Prendre (To Take) Apprendre (To Learn) Comprendre (To Understand)
Je / J' Je prends (झ़् प्रों) J'apprends (झ़ाप्रों) Je comprends (झ़् कॉंप्रों)
Tu Tu prends (त्यू प्रों) Tu apprends (त्यू अप्रों) Tu comprends (त्यू कॉंप्रों)
Il / Elle Il prend (इल प्रों) Il apprend (इल अप्रों) Elle comprend (एल कॉंप्रों)
Nous Nous prenons (नू प्रनों) Nous apprenons (नू ज़ाप्रनों) Nous comprenons (नू कॉंप्रनों)
Vous Vous prenez (वू प्रने) Vous apprenez (वू ज़ाप्रने) Vous comprenez (वू कॉंप्रने)
Ils / Elles Ils prennent (इल प्रैन) Ils apprennent (इल ज़ाप्रैन) Elles comprennent (एल कॉंप्रैन)
